(a) copper
(c) aluminium

(a) crystal of iodine
(c) piece of coal

(d) zinc granule

metal?
(a) iron nail

(a) iron

(a) mercury
(c) aluminium

(d) sodium

Which one of the following metals does not react with dilute
hydrochloric acid?
(a) magnesium
(c) iron
(b) aluminium
(d) copper
